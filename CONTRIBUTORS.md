# Contributors

- [@Skadgar](https://github.com/Skadgar): in-app updates on Android 9/10 (#12), trip planning on Android 8/9 with TLS 1.3 (#14)
- [@t-shock](https://github.com/t-shock): [lionCar](https://github.com/t-shock/lionCar-iOS), the iPhone port of LionFlow. Several ideas came back from it: all car data, the customizable home screen, fuel cost and fuel statistics, the parked card, the notes on Home and warnings from the car
